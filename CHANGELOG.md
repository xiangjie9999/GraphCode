## 0.1.13 (20 November 2026), Unreal Engine 5.7.4, Win64

- **Garment:** band wind, cut lips, `hang()` solver words and `spacing=`, `extend(..., over)`,
  `band(ends=)`, settle guide hem lift, curtains.
- Move, Shape and Core updates.
- Shape presets now come from the Market; built preset meshes are no longer bundled, so the download is smaller.
